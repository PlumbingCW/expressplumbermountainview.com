Phone, email and address

Phone: (650) 860-1670, open 24/7 for emergencies and for scheduling planned work.

Email: contact@expressplumbermountainview.com for non-urgent questions. For anything involving water, sewage or gas, phone instead.

Address: 1040 Grant Road, Mountain View, CA 94040. This is a dispatch address, so please call before you drive over.

What to have ready when you call

  • The street address of the problem and the nearest cross street.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Whether you have already shut off the main water valve or the water heater.
  • The approximate year the house was built and whether it sits on a slab.
  • A good call-back number in case the line drops.

If gas is involved, leave the building first and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us for the plumbing repair.
