Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you use this website or call our phone line.

Home › Privacy Policy

Information we collect

This website has no forms, accounts or checkout. When you call (650) 860-1670, we collect the details you share on the call, such as your name, the service address, a call-back number and a description of the problem.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the person who answers at the start of the call.

How we use it

We use call details to dispatch a plumber, confirm the appointment, handle billing questions and improve how the line is run. Recordings are used to check call quality and train the people who answer.

We do not sell the personal information you give us on a call.

Website technology

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded map from Google Maps. Those services may receive technical data such as your IP address when the page loads. We do not run advertising pixels or user accounts on this site.

Your choices and contact

You can ask what we hold about your call, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Email contact@expressplumbermountainview.com and include the phone number you called from.

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top of the sitemap-listed pages reflects the latest version.

Questions about privacy?

Email contact@expressplumbermountainview.com or call (650) 860-1670.
