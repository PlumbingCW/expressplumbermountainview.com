Emergency Hydro Jetting in Mountain View, CA

Hydro jetting pushes a controlled stream of high-pressure water through a drain line to strip away grease, scale and sludge that a cable only punches through. It is the answer when a drain keeps clogging because the pipe wall itself is coated.

We dispatch jetting around the clock for main-line backups, and book planned jetting for kitchens and older lines that clog on a schedule.

When a jetting call cannot wait

A main line that is full to the cleanout, sewage in a floor drain or a restaurant kitchen line that has stopped on a service night all qualify. So does a line that was cabled today and is already backing up again.

While you wait, stop sending water down the line. If the cleanout cap is under pressure from standing sewage, leave it closed until the plumber arrives. Opening it can release sewage across a yard or garage floor.

What jetting does that a cable does not

A cable is a spinning blade on a flexible shaft. It cuts a hole through a blockage and leaves most of the wall build-up in place. Jetting uses a hose with a forward and rear-facing nozzle. Water pressure pulls the hose down the line while the rear jets scour the wall as it is retrieved.

That makes jetting well suited to grease that has hardened into a lining, mineral scale, sludge and fine root hairs. It is not a repair. If the pipe is cracked, offset or collapsed, jetting can wash debris past the damage but cannot fix it, which is why we pair it with a camera inspection when the history of the line is unclear.

How a jetting visit runs

  1. Confirm the cause. If the line is unknown, the plumber runs a camera first. Jetting a cracked clay pipe can make a bad pipe worse.
  2. Set up access. The jetter hose goes in at the cleanout and the plumber checks the hose reaches the blockage.
  3. Jet in passes. The nozzle is worked forward, then pulled back slowly so the rear jets scour the wall. Several passes are normal on a heavily coated line.
  4. Verify. Water is run to confirm flow. If you want proof, a follow-up camera pass shows the clean wall.

Where jetting pays off for businesses

A restaurant or food service line sees grease every day, and a line that is cabled monthly can often be jetted twice a year for less overall cost and fewer shutdowns. Shops and offices with a single old lateral can use the same logic. See commercial plumbing for after-hours response and grease handling.

What shapes the price of hydro jetting

  • Line length and diameter. A short 4-inch lateral is a smaller job than a long 6-inch run.
  • Access point. A clear cleanout is quick. A buried or missing one adds time.
  • How built-up the line is. Heavy grease needs extra passes and more water.
  • Camera follow-up. A before and after look is a separate line item and optional.
  • Time of call. Emergency dispatch costs more than a booked daytime visit.

Jetting is usually priced higher than a cable pass because it needs a larger machine and more time. It is worth it when the same line has stopped more than once. The plumber gives you the price before the hose goes in.

Old laterals and roots in Mountain View

The City's wastewater FAQ puts the lateral, the pipe from your house to the City main, on the property owner. In older Mountain View neighborhoods that pipe is often clay or cast iron laid decades ago, and roots find the joints. Jetting clears root hairs and the grease that traps them, but a pipe with open joints is a repair question. Ask for a camera look if the roots return within a season.

The plumber works to the California Plumbing Code when opening a cleanout or adding one, and IAPMO, which publishes that code, also publishes the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

Hydro Jetting questions

Is emergency hydro jetting available at night in Mountain View?

Yes. Call (650) 860-1670 at any hour. A licensed plumber is dispatched for a main-line backup, and the plumber decides on site whether to cable first or jet.

Can jetting damage my pipes?

On a sound pipe, no. On a cracked, offset or old clay pipe, high pressure can open a weak spot. That is why a camera check comes first when the line's condition is unknown.

How often should a kitchen line be jetted?

It depends on the kitchen. A busy restaurant may need it every few months, a home kitchen with a recurring grease clog often twice a year. The plumber can suggest a schedule after seeing the line.

Does jetting remove tree roots?

It clears fine roots and root hairs well. Thick woody roots may need a cutting head first. Roots that return mean the pipe has an opening, and the opening is the real fix.

Will jetting make a mess?

The plumber contains the water at the cleanout and cleans the area afterward. Expect some wet ground around the cleanout.
