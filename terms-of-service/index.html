Using this website

The pages on this site are general information about plumbing in Mountain View, California. They are not a substitute for a licensed plumber's inspection of your property, and they do not create a service agreement.

Phone dispatch and pricing

Service starts with a phone call. The plumber diagnoses the problem on site and gives you a clear price before work starts. Work proceeds only after you approve the price.
