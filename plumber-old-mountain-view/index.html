Where Old Mountain View is

The Old Mountain View Neighborhood Association describes its area as bordered by El Camino Real, Shoreline Boulevard, Evelyn Avenue and Highways 85 and 237. It contains more than 2,400 residences and the downtown business district.

Castro Street is the main corridor, with restaurants and cafes. Pioneer Memorial Park is near the Center for the Performing Arts, and Eagle Park has a large field and an outdoor pool.

The association's own description of the housing is short: grand Victorian homes and quaint cottages. It gives no construction dates, so this page does not claim a build year for any street.

Why older homes ask different plumbing questions

This section is general plumbing information. Houses with a long history often have a mix of original and replaced plumbing. Over decades, rooms get added, kitchens are moved and pipes are patched, so the material behind one wall may not match the next.

The way to learn what you have is to look, not to guess. A sewer camera inspection shows the condition of the lateral, and a plumber can check visible supply lines for material and wear. If supply pipe turns out to be failing, repiping is the permanent answer.

Slow drains in older kitchens and baths often come from buildup in a long, many-bend run. Drain cleaning clears it, and a plumber can tell you if something stronger is needed when cabling keeps failing.
