Where Cuernavaca is

A December 2015 Mountain View Voice profile places Cuernavaca off Crestview Drive near the Sunnyvale border, about one block from El Camino Real. It has two entrances and a main circle with five cul-de-sacs.

The community has about 170 Spanish-style homes on roughly 30 acres, with red roofs and floor plans from 1,500 to 2,500 square feet. The land was a cherry orchard, then the nine-hole Cherry Chase Golf Course and a swim club.

Newer homes, different questions

Residents began moving in around August 1988 and the community was finished in 1989 in five phases. That makes it far newer than the 1950s tracts elsewhere in Mountain View.

This is general plumbing information. A house from the late 1980s is more likely to have copper or plastic supply lines than older materials, but this site cannot say what any Cuernavaca home actually has. Ask a plumber to identify it. Water heaters of that age are often at or beyond their typical service life, so a leak or rust-colored hot water is a reason to look at water heater replacement.

Multi-level homes with several bathrooms can hide a slow leak behind a wall or ceiling. Leak detection finds it without opening everything, and toilet repair and faucet repair cover the common small fixes.
