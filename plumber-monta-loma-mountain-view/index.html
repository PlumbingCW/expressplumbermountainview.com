Where Monta Loma is and how it was built

Monta Loma is bounded by San Antonio Road, Middlefield Road, Rengstorff Avenue and Central Expressway. Its homes were built in the post-war housing boom, and Wikipedia's neighborhood entry credits three builders: Joseph Eichler, John Calder Mackay and the Mardell Building Company.

Each builder named its own sections. Eichler's tracts are called Oakwood and Fairview, and Mardell's is Mardell Manor. The neighborhood is also noted for one of the best-preserved tracts of Mackay homes, and the Monta Loma Neighborhood Association has been active since 1977.

Within the neighborhood, then, there are three different builders and three different sets of construction habits. That is the main reason one Monta Loma address can behave differently from the house a few blocks away.

What the mid-century construction usually means for plumbing

This part is general plumbing information, not a survey of individual Monta Loma homes. Many mid-century California houses were built on concrete slabs, which puts some water supply lines inside or under the floor. Eichler homes are also known for radiant floor heating in some tracts, and that heating pipe can fail on its own schedule.

A warm patch on the floor, the sound of water when every tap is off, or a water bill that climbs with no change in habits all point toward a hidden leak. Start with leak detection, and if the evidence points under the floor, read about slab leak repair.

Older drain lines run under the slab as well, so a repeat clog on a ground-floor drain deserves a camera look before more cabling. See sewer camera inspection and drain cleaning.

Water heaters and fixtures in older Monta Loma homes

Water heaters in homes of this era often sit in a garage or utility closet. Moving one is a venting and plumbing project, not a swap, so ask about it before you choose a new unit. The City of Mountain View requires a permit for water heater replacement, which a legitimate job should include.

Weigh water heater replacement against a simple repair before deciding. If the tank itself is leaking, repair is usually not an option.

A note on Monta Loma

Monta Loma was also the site of an Ohlone village and the Castro Indian Mound, which was leveled in 1947. In 1989 Stanford University returned remains and artifacts to Ohlone descendants. This is background only and has no effect on a plumbing repair today.
